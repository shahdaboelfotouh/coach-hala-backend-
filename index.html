My Story

Hala El Shahawy

Certified Life Coach · ICF-Accredited Provider

I believe that every person carries within them the clarity, wisdom, and strength they are searching for. My role as a coach is not to give you answers — it's to create the space where you can hear your own.

Trained and certified through Wisdom Coaching School, an ICF-Accredited Provider, I bring a rigorous, evidence-based approach to coaching that is also deeply human — attuned to the cultural realities, pressures, and dreams of people in Egypt and the Arab world.

Over 200+ client journeys, I've walked alongside professionals navigating career crossroads, individuals rebuilding from burnout, young adults finding their footing, and people learning to truly communicate. Each story is unique. Each transformation is real.

My Coaching Philosophy

  • Coaching is a partnership — you are the expert on your own life.
  • Lasting change comes from insight, not advice.
  • Your pace is the right pace — there's no rush in real growth.
  • Honesty, confidentiality, and deep respect are non-negotiable.
  • Every session should leave you more resourceful than when you arrived.

Policies

Terms, Policies & Confidentiality

Please read these carefully before booking. You'll agree to them at checkout.

1. Cancellation & Rescheduling Policy

⚠ All clients must explicitly agree to this policy before payment is processed.

  • More than 24 hours before the session: Full refund or free reschedule.
  • 2–24 hours before the session: Reschedule only — no monetary refund.
  • Within 30 minutes OR no-show: Coach retains 50% of the total session fee. Remaining 50% refunded or credited at coach's discretion.

This policy applies to both full-payment and 50%-deposit bookings. Bundle sessions cancelled without adequate notice still consume one session from the bundle.

2. Refund Policy

Eligible refunds are processed within 5–10 business days to the original payment method. InstaPay refunds are sent to the registered IPA/phone number on file.

3. Deposit & Balance Payments

Clients selecting the 50% deposit agree to pay the remaining balance at least 24 hours before the session. Unpaid balances result in session cancellation with no refund of the deposit. Reminder emails are sent 48 and 24 hours before the session.

4. Confidentiality

All information shared in sessions is strictly confidential. Coach Hala will not disclose any personal information to third parties without explicit written consent, except where required by law. Coaching is not a substitute for therapy or medical treatment.

5. Session Conduct

Sessions beginning late due to the client will end at the scheduled time and cannot be extended. Maximum session length is 2.5 hours regardless of format.

6. Zoom Sessions

Clients are responsible for a stable internet connection and a private environment. Recording sessions without mutual consent is prohibited.

7. Privacy & Data

Personal data is collected solely for managing bookings and communications, stored securely, and never sold. Request deletion at any time: halalife81@gmail.com

Questions about these policies? Don't hesitate to reach out.
